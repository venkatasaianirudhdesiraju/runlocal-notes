## Choosing the Right Hardware for Self-Hosted AI

Self-hosting AI models at home can be a game-changer for developers and researchers. However, it requires careful consideration of the underlying hardware to ensure smooth performance. In this post, we'll explore the essential hardware requirements for running 7B-13B local models comfortably at home.

### CPU: A Well-Balanced Choice

For self-hosted AI, you'll need a CPU that can handle matrix operations efficiently. A high clock speed is not as crucial as a high number of cores. We recommend looking for a CPU with at least 6-8 cores and a base clock speed of 2.5 GHz or higher. Some examples of CPUs that fit this bill are:

* AMD Ryzen 5 5600X
* Intel Core i5-11600K

### RAM: 32 GB and Beyond

RAM is essential for self-hosted AI, as it needs to handle large datasets and model weights. For 7B-13B local models, we recommend a minimum of 32 GB of DDR4 RAM. However, if you plan to work with larger models or run multiple instances, 64 GB or more is a better choice. Look for RAM with a speed of at least 3200 MHz.

### VRAM: 8 GB and Up

A dedicated graphics card (GPU) is necessary for self-hosted AI, as it handles the compute-intensive matrix operations. For 7B-13B local models, we recommend a GPU with at least 8 GB of VRAM. Some examples of suitable GPUs are:

* NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060
* AMD Radeon RX 6700 XT

### Storage: A Fast NVMe SSD

Self-hosted AI requires fast storage to load models and datasets quickly. We recommend a fast NVMe SSD with a capacity of at least 1 TB. Some examples of suitable SSDs are:

* Samsung 970 EVO Plus
* WD Black SN750

### Mini PC vs Tower: A Balanced Approach

When it comes to choosing a form factor, a mini PC is a great option for self-hosted AI. They're compact, energy-efficient, and often more affordable than their tower counterparts. Some examples of mini PCs that fit our hardware requirements are:

* Intel NUC 10
* ASRock J5005B-ITX

However, if you prefer a more traditional tower build, look for a case with good airflow and cable management to keep your system cool and organized.

### Power Supply: A Sufficient Unit

A sufficient power supply unit (PSU) is essential to power your system. Look for a PSU with a sufficient wattage rating (at least 650 W) to handle the power requirements of your hardware.

### Assembling Your System

When assembling your system, consider the following tips:

* Use a well-ventilated case to keep your system cool.
* Install the GPU and RAM securely to prevent overheating and data loss.
* Use a high-quality power supply unit to ensure stable power delivery.

### Conclusion

Self-hosting AI at home requires careful consideration of the underlying hardware. By following our guidelines, you can build a system that comfortably runs 7B-13B local models. Remember to balance your components, prioritize performance over raw power, and keep your system well-cooled. Happy building!
